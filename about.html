From Sunderland to the World

KidsDev began in Sunderland, UK, with a simple but powerful idea: technology education should be accessible, engaging, and industry-relevant for every child, regardless of background.

We recognized a gap. While children are digital natives, true digital literacy—the ability to create rather than just consume—was rare. We set out to bridge this gap by introducing professional-grade tools like Python, Linux, and GitHub to young minds early.

Today, we have evolved into a Global Academy with a footprint in the UK and Nigeria. We offer free open-source resources, intensive bootcamps, and expert mentorship that rivals university-level introductions.

Our Core Values

The pillars of our pedagogy.

Accessibility First

Quality education shouldn't have a paywall. We provide extensive free resources to ensure no child is left behind.

Creativity Over Syntax

We teach logic and problem-solving first. Memorizing code is secondary to understanding how to build solutions.

Safe Environment

A supportive, bully-free zone where making mistakes is celebrated as an essential part of the learning process.
