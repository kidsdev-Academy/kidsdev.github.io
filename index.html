Frequently Asked Questions 🙋‍♂️

Is KidsDev Academy free?

All our self-paced resources and basic tutorials are 100% free! We also offer premium mentored classes for deeper guidance.

What age is appropriate?

Ages 6-9 should start with Computer Basics, while ages 10-16 can dive into Python or Web Dev.

Do I need a powerful computer?

No! Any basic laptop or desktop (Windows, Mac, or Linux) works perfectly. Even a Chromebook is fine for our Web and Scratch courses.
