Why Start with Scratch?

Scratch is designed by MIT to help young minds learn logic without the frustration of typing complex code.

No Typing Errors

Forget missing semicolons! Blocks snap together like LEGO. If it fits, it works.

Instant Creativity

Draw your own characters, record your own sounds, and build your own worlds instantly.

Real Coding Logic

Learn loops, variables, and conditions. The same concepts used by professional engineers.
