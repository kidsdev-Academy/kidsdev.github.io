# 🚀 KidsDev Academy: The Global Tech Mentorship Group

> **"Don't just play games. Build them."**

**KidsDev Academy** is a premier online technology institution dedicated to democratizing tech education for children aged 6–16. From our roots in Sunderland to a global digital classroom, we are empowering the next generation of problem solvers, innovators, and leaders.

🔗 **[Visit the Live Site](https://thekidsdev.netlify.app)**

---

## 🌍 About The Mission

We believe that true digital literacy goes beyond swiping screens. It’s about understanding the logic behind the magic.

Our curriculum is designed to take students from **"Zero to Junior Developer"**. We don't teach syntax; we teach logic, creativity, and problem-solving using professional-grade tools like **Python**, **Linux**, **GitHub**, and **Cyber Security protocols**.

---

## 💎 What We Offer

### 1. 🎓 Premium Mentorship Programs
We offer four distinct, intensive tracks designed to fit every schedule:

* **📅 Weekend Coding Club:** A consistent 9-week mentorship program running every Saturday & Sunday. The perfect long-term growth path.
* **☀️ Summer Code Camp:** A legendary 4-week intensive holiday experience. Master a new language before school resumes.
* **🐰 Easter Tech Bootcamp:** A 1-week "Sprint" focused on completing a specific project (Game or Website) in just 5 days.
* **❄️ Winter Game Jam:** The ultimate end-of-year global hackathon. 48 hours. Teams form. Games are built. Prizes are won.

### 2. 📺 The Free Learning Library
We believe education should be accessible. Our **Resources Hub** features a curated, dynamically updated library of free video tutorials covering:
* Python & Pygame
* Web Development (HTML/CSS)
* Computer Basics & Hardware
* Tech Tips & Tricks

### 3. 🛡️ Global Virtual Classroom
* **100% Online:** Join from Lagos, London, Dubai, or New York.
* **Live Mentorship:** No pre-recorded boredom. Real-time interaction with expert instructors.
* **Safe Community:** A vetted, bully-free environment for kids to collaborate.
